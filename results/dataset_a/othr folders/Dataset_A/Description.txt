AVAILABLE METADATA

The publicly available dataset primarily provides MRI images and their corresponding diagnostic class labels.

Consistent patient-level and clinical metadata were not available across all source datasets. Therefore, the following information is not consistently available:

- Patient ID
- Patient age
- Patient sex
- Clinical history
- Hospital/institution information
- MRI scanner manufacturer
- Magnetic field strength
- MRI acquisition parameters/protocols

No unavailable patient-level or clinical metadata were generated or assumed.
